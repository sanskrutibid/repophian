Healthcare Campaign Case Study

A Conversation Worth Having

NSH Hospital’s approach to breast cancer awareness

ResultTop rankings on every search engine, 5X the social engagement of the previous year, and a steep rise in sales.

IndustryHealthcare & Wellness
Services ProvidedSocial Media Strategy • Creative Direction • Healthcare Campaigns • Educational Communication

Overview & Challenge

Some conversations deserve more than a social media post. Breast cancer awareness is one of them.

For NSH Hospital, the focus was to bring an important health subject into everyday digital conversations through clear, informative and visually engaging communication.

Our Strategy: Three Pillars of Communication

The communication framework was built around three core strategic pillars.

Inform
Turn important medical topics into simple, accessible and reader-friendly content.
Engage
Use relevant questions, such as “Who Should Consider Screening?”, to spark curiosity and action.
Build Trust
Bring doctors into the communication to connect general awareness with genuine medical expertise.

Campaign Execution

  • Targeted Creative Design: Developed focused breast cancer awareness visual creatives.
  • Educational Content: Created clear instructional material centred around mammography and routine screening.
  • Doctor-Led Credibility: Featured Dr. Umesh Vinay Biyani and Dr. Nikita Mandhane Biyani directly in awareness messaging.
  • Visual Storytelling: Used structured visual narratives to make dense clinical information easily absorbable.

Clinical Expertise Featured: Dr Umesh Biyani • Dr Nikita Biyani • Dr Vaibhav Biyani • Dr Neha Biyani

The Outcome

Established a focused social media content presence that united breast cancer awareness, screening education and credible medical expertise under one cohesive communication direction.

The Key Takeaway

“Awareness gets attention. Meaningful communication makes information matter.”

For NSH Hospital, the goal was simple: make an important health conversation clearer, more accessible and harder to overlook.

A clearer health conversation, one post at a time.
